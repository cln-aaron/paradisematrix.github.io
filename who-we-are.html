Who we are

A company built for the slow part of the work.

Paradise Matrix Pte. Ltd. was established in Singapore to take agentic AI into sectors where adoption is decided by review and regulation rather than by appetite.

Our story

How we got here.

The cybersecurity group we work alongside has spent years distributing security products and providing security services to enterprises. That is an unusual vantage point. It shows you what organisations buy, what they manage to deploy, and how far apart those two things sit.

When agentic AI arrived the same pattern returned. Pilots were everywhere and production was scarce. What was missing was not capability but the apparatus that lets a security lead or a university registrar put their name to a decision.

Paradise Matrix was set up to build that apparatus and take it to market, beginning in the domain we understand best.

What we stand for

What we design against.

Four commitments, each with a corresponding decision in the product.

01

Accountable autonomy

An agent can act on its own. It cannot act without leaving a record. Decisions, tool calls and model selections are logged and attributable.

02

Data where it belongs

Your data sits where your regulator and your board expect it to. Managed cloud, your own infrastructure or entirely disconnected, on one platform.

03

Domain before scale

We work inside the practice of one sector at a time, with practitioners present, until the agent improves on what it replaces.

04

No single model

Leadership among frontier models changes often. The platform routes across several, so an architecture need not be rebuilt each time it does.
