Our story

How we got here.

Paradise Matrix is the Singapore company of Edvance International Holdings, our parent, a cybersecurity group listed on the Main Board of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong. Edvance has spent years distributing security products and running security services for enterprises across Asia. That gives the group an unusual view of the market: what organisations buy, what they manage to put into use, and how wide the gap between the two can be.

The same gap opened again with agentic AI, and the reason is worth stating plainly. Adoption is not held up by weak models. It is held up because nobody can say who is accountable when an agent acts.

A CISO will not sign off an agent that triages alerts or drafts incident summaries without a clear, tamper-proof record of what it did and why. A university will not let agents near student data, admissions or assessment unless every action can be traced, reviewed and justified. Neither objection is technical. Both come down to evidence.

RealRelay.ai treats that evidence as a core feature of the product. Every agent interaction is logged in a structured, searchable form. A security lead can reconstruct an incident and the decisions inside it. A registrar or compliance officer can audit an AI-assisted process. An institution can show its governance to a regulator, an auditor or its own board.

That accountability layer is what most organisations were missing, and it is what takes this work from pilot to production. Paradise Matrix was set up to build it and take it to market, starting in the domain we know best.

Our market

Singapore is home. We operate from the Gulf to Japan.

Singapore is where we build, sell and prove the work. Every domain launches here first, and what it achieves here sets the benchmark for the rest of the region.

Beyond Singapore, the ten ASEAN economies form a single market of more than 670 million people. Hong Kong is our parent company's base and our route into North Asia. Japan and the United Arab Emirates complete the footprint.

These markets share the conditions the platform was built for: regulated industries, data that must stay in country, and institutions accountable for the systems they run.

What we stand for

What we design against.

Four commitments, each with a matching decision in the product.

01

Accountable autonomy

An agent can act on its own, but it cannot act without leaving a record. Decisions, tool calls and model selections are logged and attributable.

02

Data where it belongs

Your data sits where your regulator and your board expect it to sit: managed cloud, your own infrastructure or fully disconnected, all on one platform.

03
