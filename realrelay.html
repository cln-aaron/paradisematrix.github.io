Capabilities

What the platform does that a model alone does not.

Every domain we build inherits these, and so does every domain a partner builds with us.

01

Routing between models

The most capable model is not required for every task. Requests are directed to a model chosen on the merits of the task, weighing speed, cost and capability, and observing rules about what may leave your environment at all.

02

Assembly without code

Describe what an agent should do and the platform helps structure it, connect the tools it needs and check it before it runs. The people who understand the workflow build the agent, which is what allows this to move beyond a pilot.

03

Governance inside the runtime

Policy, identity and audit are not a reporting layer added afterwards. They sit in the execution path: what an agent may reach, who authorised it, which model answered and what was done, recorded as it happens.

04

Security inside the platform

A validation agent runs against the agents you build, continuously and within a scope you set, returning risk ratings with evidence and remediation rather than a report at the end of a project. How that works.

05

One route to many models

Separate arrangements with separate providers, each with its own terms and invoice, become one route with one set of controls. Providers can be changed without rebuilding what sits above them.

Security, built in

The platform tests the agents you build on it.

On most platforms, building an agent and checking whether it is safe are two separate purchases. Security testing arrives as a tool you bolt on afterwards, runs once before release, and then goes stale while the agent keeps changing.

RealRelay.ai carries the validation inside it. An agent is built and examined in the same environment, so the check keeps pace with the thing being checked.

01

Testing that does not stop

An autonomous validation agent probes the agents you build, continuously rather than at a single gate before release. An agent that changes on Tuesday is examined on Tuesday.

02

Adversarial, inside your scope

Within the boundary you authorise, it reasons the way an attacker would to surface real exposures, and reports each one with a risk rating, the evidence behind it and what to do about it.

03

A loop that closes

Find, fix, re-test. Because development and verification sit in one environment, a finding becomes a fix and the fix is confirmed, wherever you run: our cloud, your infrastructure or disconnected, with your own keys and local models.

We are precise about our posture. Paradise Matrix does not currently hold formal security certifications, and we do not describe ourselves as certified for anything we have not earned. Security and compliance characteristics vary by deployment tier and are set out in your agreement with us.

Deployment

The same platform in three postures.

Which one you run should follow what your regulator and your board require, not what a vendor is able to support.

Managed cloud

Fastest to value

Run and maintained by us, with regional residency options. The usual starting point for enterprise teams.

  • Managed upgrades
  • Regional residency options
  • Elastic capacity
Your infrastructure

Inside your perimeter

The platform runs on your own infrastructure, integrated with your identity systems and with key management under your control.

  • Runs inside your network
  • Keys held by you
  • Integrates with existing identity
Disconnected

Governance and assurance

How we keep an agent inside its boundary.

Every agent works inside limits somebody set deliberately. These are the four parts of them.

01

Scoped permissions

An agent receives the tools and data its task requires and nothing adjacent. Scope is declared before deployment rather than discovered afterwards.

02

Human checkpoints

Consequential actions stop for approval. Which actions those are is your decision, set per agent and per environment.

03

A complete record

Prompt, plan, tool call, model selected, output and approver, held in sequence and exportable for review months later.

04

Checks before release

New agents are evaluated against policy before they run, a gate that applies equally to a student's first assistant and a production security agent.
