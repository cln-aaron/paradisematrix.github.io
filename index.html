Our position

The constraint on enterprise AI is no longer capability. It is the record.

Recent years produced remarkable models and comparatively few systems in production. The obstacle we meet in the field is rarely whether a model can perform a task. It is whether the organisation can later establish who authorised the agent, what data it reached, which model answered and on what basis.

Until those questions have answers, an AI programme remains a pilot. Paradise Matrix exists to supply them, in the sectors where they are asked most insistently.

Agentic AIThe same trick that fools a person

An attacker no longer has to break in

Give an agent your inbox and your files and there is nothing to breach. An attacker simply leaves instructions the agent will read and obey. That is the specific failure RealRelay.ai was built to catch, by testing agents continuously inside the platform rather than trusting a request because it looks legitimate.

IdentityOn identifiers that leak

Once a number can be looked up, it proves nothing

Identifiers were treated as secrets long after they stopped being secret. The design response is to assume they will leak, and to make knowing them nowhere near enough to get in. On the platform that means least privilege between every agent, model and dataset.

ResilienceOn the telco outage

Resilience is the switchover you have practised

Standing up an impressive agent is straightforward. Proving it fails safely when something breaks is the hard part, and the part that matters. The question we keep asking is not only whether it works, but what happens on its worst day.

Common questions

Questions we are asked most.

Plain answers about what we build, how it is deployed and what we do and do not claim.

What is agentic AI?

Agentic AI describes software that can perceive its context, plan a sequence of steps, use tools and carry out multi-step tasks with limited human intervention, rather than only answering a single prompt.

What does Paradise Matrix do?

Paradise Matrix is a Singapore company that builds and commercialises agentic AI for organisations that have to account for what their systems do. It develops the RealRelay.ai platform and builds vertical agents, starting with cybersecurity and education.

What is RealRelay.ai?

RealRelay.ai is a platform for creating, governing and deploying AI agents without writing code. It reaches several third-party models through one route, selects between them per task, records what each agent does, and runs on managed cloud, on customer infrastructure, or fully disconnected.

How is security handled in the platform?

Security validation is carried inside the platform rather than bought separately. An autonomous validation agent continuously tests the agents built on the platform within a scope the customer authorises, reasons the way an attacker would to surface real exposures, and returns a risk rating with supporting evidence and remediation guidance, then re-tests once a fix is made.

Can the platform run without sending data outside our environment?

Yes. On on-premises and air-gapped deployments the platform runs open-weight models locally with no outbound telemetry, and customers can hold their own keys. This does not apply to the multi-model cloud tier, which routes to third-party model providers.

Which sectors does Paradise Matrix work in?

Three domains. Cybersecurity covers alert triage, adversary simulation, monitoring, incident response support and validation of the agents themselves. Education covers a learning environment where students build agents for their own discipline alongside their lecturers, with classroom and academic performance signals turned into insight for educators. Fintech covers decision intelligence on investment cases and performance, anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer review, and entity research into who sits behind a counterparty.

Where does Paradise Matrix operate?

Singapore is the home and reference market. The footprint covers thirteen markets: the ten ASEAN economies, Hong Kong, Japan and the United Arab Emirates. Each domain is proved in Singapore and then localised for the jurisdiction it enters.

Does Paradise Matrix hold security certifications?

No. Paradise Matrix does not currently hold formal security certifications and does not describe itself as certified for anything it has not earned. Security and compliance characteristics vary by deployment tier and are set out in the customer agreement.
