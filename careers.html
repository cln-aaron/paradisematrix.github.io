How we hire

Four conversations, about two weeks.

  • Introduction, thirty minutes. What you have built, what you want next, and an honest account of where we actually are.
  • Deep dive, ninety minutes. A real problem from our roadmap. No algorithm trivia and no exercise that consumes your weekend.
  • The team, one hour. Meet the people you would work with, and ask them what you would not ask a hiring manager.
  • Decision. Within three working days of the last conversation. We will tell you either way.
