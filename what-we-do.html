What we do

Three domains, built one at a time until the work is genuinely better.

We build around industries rather than features. We take a field, learn how its work is really done, and build agents that carry weight in it. Three of those fields are furthest along today.

Domain 01

Cybersecurity

A security operations centre produces more alerts in a day than its analysts can read in a week. The customary response is more tooling, which produces more alerts. Agentic AI helps here only if it can be trusted to act, and trust in this setting means bounded, logged and open to review.

Our agents take the parts of the job that do not scale with headcount. They read the alert, gather the context an analyst would have gathered, form a view, and hand over a decision rather than a queue.

01

Triage

Alerts assessed, enriched and ranked before an analyst opens them, with false positives closed against a reasoned trail rather than silently suppressed.

02

Adversary simulation

Safe, continuous emulation of attacker behaviour, so a control that has drifted is found in the week it drifts rather than at the annual test.

03

Monitoring and analysis

Sustained watch across signals and sources, with analysis written for the person who has to make the call on it.

04

Response support

Timelines assembled, containment options drafted and reporting written while an incident is still live, each action attributable.

Securing the agents themselves

Defending an organisation with agents raises an obvious question: who defends the agents. The platform answers it directly, with a validation agent that tests what is built on it, inside a scope the customer sets, and reports findings with the evidence behind them.

Domain 02

Education

From the 2027 academic year, students across Singapore's institutes of higher learning are expected to receive compulsory AI skills training. That turns a procurement question into a curriculum one: what do students actually build, who supervises it, and what does the institution learn from it.

We run a learning environment rather than a licence. Students build agents for the discipline they are actually studying, lecturers work in the same space rather than reviewing at submission, and what the cohort produces feeds back to the people teaching it.

The analytics are built for differentiated instruction: seeing which students are genuinely keeping up, and directing classroom time where it will do the most good.

01

Built in their own discipline

Students across faculties build agents for the field they are studying, whether that is fintech, manufacturing or marketing communications. The AI work is the coursework, not a module bolted beside it.

02

Lecturers in the same space

Educators work alongside the cohort in the same environment, shaping a project while it is still taking form rather than marking it once it is finished.

03

Signals a teacher can act on

Classroom recordings and academic performance data become insight for the educator, built on established pedagogical practice rather than presented as a dashboard to interpret.

04

The institution keeps it

What a cohort creates stays with the institution, retained and retrievable long after the students have graduated, with the record of how it was used.

Domain 03

Fintech

Capital moves on judgement made under time pressure, and every automated step behind that judgement has to be explainable to a regulator afterwards. That is the condition the platform was built for.

The work splits two ways: helping people read a situation before they commit to it, and carrying the review burden that sits behind every client and every transaction.

01

Decision intelligence

Investment cases and sales performance taken apart line by line, so an investor or an operator gets a clearer read on what the numbers actually say before capital is committed.

02

AML and KYC

Anti-money-laundering alerts and know-your-customer checks assessed, enriched and ranked before a reviewer opens them, with the reasoning recorded so the decision can be defended later.

03

Entity research

Organisational research that maps who actually sits behind a counterparty, and the risk that travels with them, assembled from the sources an analyst would otherwise work through by hand.

04

Data that stays put

Deployment inside the institution’s own environment, with customer-held keys, for firms whose data cannot leave the jurisdiction.

02
